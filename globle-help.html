🌍 How to Play Globle

Two games, one spinning globe, and all 195 countries of the world. Pick your game on the start screen.

🔥

Hot & Cold

Find the mystery country using nothing but colours.

🎯 The basics

  1. Guess any country. Type its name, or tap it right on the globe. Anywhere is fine for your first guess. It's just a starting point.
  2. Read the colour. Your guess lights up on the globe. Pale yellow means you're miles off. Deep red means you're right next door.
  3. Check the distance. Your guesses are listed from closest to furthest, with the exact distance in km.
  4. Close in. Use the colours to steer your next guess, getting warmer each time.
  5. Find it! Guess the mystery country and it turns green. Cue confetti. 🎉

There's no guess limit. Take as many as you need. Fewer is just more impressive.

Countries you haven't guessed stay plain grey. The lighter grey bits (like Greenland, Taiwan or Antarctica) aren't among the 195, so they can't be guessed.

📏 How distance works

Distance is measured from the closest edge of one country to the closest edge of the other, not centre to centre. Any country that shares a border with the answer shows as Bordering (0 km), the hottest possible colour.

Watch out for overseas territories. A country's far-flung islands and territories count as part of it. France owns French Guiana in South America, so France "borders" Brazil. Spain's cities in North Africa mean Spain borders Morocco. Use this to your advantage, or get delightfully confused by it.

🎮 Daily or Unlimited

📅

Daily

One mystery country per day, the same one for everyone. Compare scores with friends. It resets at midnight and every country comes up once before any repeat.

♾️

Unlimited

A fresh random country every round. Play as many as you like, perfect for practice or a long train ride.

💡 Playing with hints

Choose With hints on the start screen and you can unlock up to three hints as you go. Each one opens after a certain number of guesses, and you decide whether to actually reveal it.

🔍 Tiny countries

Some countries are too small to tap on a globe, like Vatican City, Tuvalu and Singapore. They get a little dot instead. White dots haven't been guessed yet, and coloured dots have.

Tap any dot (or any country) and a magnifier pops up with a close-up of it and its name. Hit Guess to lock it in, or Close if you were just having a nosy look.

🧠 Hot & Cold tips

Open big. Start with a large, central country like Russia, Brazil, China or Australia. A big country gives you a much more useful first reading.

Triangulate. Two guesses with similar distances on opposite sides narrow it down fast. The answer is somewhere in between.

Bordering means neighbour. If something turns deep red, the answer touches it. List its neighbours and work through them.

Remember the islands. If you're stuck with red all around an ocean, the answer might be an island nation. Check for dots.

📍

Name it

A country lights up. You name it. Simple… right?

🎯 How it works

  1. Pick your settings. Choose how many countries you need to find (5, 10, 20, 30 or 50) and how many lives you get (1 to 5 hearts).
  2. A country lights up purple. The globe spins over and zooms in on it. Tiny countries get a pulsing purple ring so you can't miss them.
  3. Name it! Get it right and it turns green, you score +1 and a burst of confetti goes off.
  4. Get it wrong and a heart breaks. 💔 The real answer is revealed and you move on to the next country.
  5. Win by finding all your countries before your hearts run out. Lose every heart and it's game over.

Your map fills up as you play: soft green for the ones you nailed, soft red for the ones that got away.

⌨️

Start typing and the list filters down as you go, or tap the box to scroll through all 195 countries and pick one. Totally stumped? Hit No idea to skip, though it still costs a heart.

❤️ Hearts, streaks & stars

❤️❤️💔
Every wrong answer breaks a heart. Lose them all and the globe fades to grey. Game over.

🔥 Streaks: get 3 or more right in a row and a flame counter appears. Your best run is saved in your stats.

⭐ Stars: win without losing a single heart for 3 stars. Lose a few and you'll get 2 or 1 stars.

📊 Stats & streaks

Tap 📊 any time to see your stats. There are three tabs: Daily, Unlimited and Name it.

Hot & Cold tracks games played, win rate, average and fewest guesses, and a chart of how many guesses you usually need.
Daily streak: solve the daily country on consecutive days to keep it alive. Miss a day or give up and it's back to zero.
Unlimited streak: win rounds in a row without giving up.

Name it tracks games played, win rate, perfect games, accuracy, your best run and how many countries you've identified in total.

❓ Quick questions

Which countries are in the game?

All 195 recognised countries: the 193 UN members plus Vatican City and Palestine. Territories like Greenland, Puerto Rico and Hong Kong aren't separate answers.

It doesn't recognise the name I typed

Most common names work, like "USA", "UK", "Holland", "Burma", "Ivory Coast", "DRC" and "Czech Republic". If you're unsure, start typing and choose from the suggestions.

Why is a country far away showing as "Bordering"?

It probably owns a territory near the answer. France, the UK, the Netherlands, the US and Denmark all have far-flung bits.

Why can't I tap countries in Name it?

Because tapping shows a country's name, and that would be cheating! 😉 You can still spin and zoom the globe to get a better look.

Are tiny countries in Name it too?

Yes, all 195 are fair game, from Russia to Vatican City. Small ones get a pulsing purple ring so you can spot them.

Can I replay today's daily?

Each daily can only be played once, but you can check your result anytime. Want more? Unlimited has you covered.
